🛒 Ordering & Pricing

Browse our products, click Order via WhatsApp on any product page, and we'll confirm your order in minutes. You can also add multiple items to your cart and proceed with instant WhatsApp checkout. For a complete walkthrough, see our How to Order Guide.
Can I negotiate prices or request bulk discounts?
Yes! We are open to reasonable price discussions, especially for multiple device purchases, bundle deals, or corporate tech procurement. Reach out to our WhatsApp sales line with your offer and we'll do our best to work out a competitive deal.
How do I verify real-time stock availability?
Items marked In Stock on the website are active in our inventory. Because high-demand flagships sell rapidly, our WhatsApp concierge will confirm serials, storage tiers, and exact colors for you immediately before payment.
Can I order via direct telephone call?
Yes, call us directly on 08083873316. However, WhatsApp is recommended as it allows us to share device unboxing clips, IMEI/serial verifications, and digital invoices.

🚚 Delivery & Pickup Logistics

How fast is delivery within Ibadan?
Within Ibadan: Same day, dispatched in under 3 hours to Bodija, UI, Ring Road, Akobo, Oluyole, Jericho, and all environs. Our dispatch rider will contact you prior to arrival.

Absolutely. You are welcome to visit our physical showroom at No 19, Omoola Plaza, Iwo Road, Ibadan. You can inspect the packaging, test battery health, and verify camera quality before completing payment.
