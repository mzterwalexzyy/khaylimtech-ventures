How to Order

Home How to Order

Ordering from KhaylimTech is simple. Browse our store, pick what you love, and reach us via WhatsApp or walk in at our Ibadan store. We'll handle the rest.

1

Browse & Pick Your Product

Explore our full catalogue on the website. You can filter by category — Phones, Laptops, Gaming, or Accessories — or use the search bar to find something specific. Click any product to view full details and price.

2

Add to Cart or Chat Directly

Click Add to Cart to save items, then proceed to checkout — or skip the cart and click Order via WhatsApp on any product page to send us your enquiry instantly. WhatsApp is always the fastest way to confirm availability and negotiate.

3

Confirm Your Order

Our team will confirm stock availability, your delivery address, and final pricing. For price negotiation, just let us know on WhatsApp — we're always open to a deal.

4

Make Payment

We accept bank transfer and cash on delivery (within Ibadan only). Payment details will be shared after your order is confirmed. You'll receive a confirmation once payment is received.

5

Delivery or Pickup

Choose home delivery or pick up at our store in Iwo Road, Ibadan. Deliveries within Ibadan are completed same day or within 24 hours. Orders outside Oyo State are delivered in 1–3 business days via courier.
